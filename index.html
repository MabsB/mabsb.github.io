03 — About

Practical builder, steady learner

I’m Bernabe, a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at National University – Fairview, specializing in Mobile and Internet Technology. I’ve been a consistent Dean’s Lister, and I expect to graduate in 2027.

My hands-on experience comes from academic and project work: wiring an ESP32 and a UHF RFID reader to a Firebase web app, shipping a live e-commerce site behind Cloudflare, and debugging Bluetooth between an Android app and an Arduino.

Beyond projects, I’ve completed Cisco Networking Academy courses in networking and cybersecurity, worked with REST APIs, and explored AI-assisted development, YOLO object detection, and n8n workflow automation. I’m looking for an onsite internship where I can troubleshoot real systems and contribute to a technical team.
